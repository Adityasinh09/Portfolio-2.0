## Overview

Developed a Python-based data cleaning pipeline and built a comprehensive Tableau dashboard to visualize Netflix's 2014–2019 "Content Explosion" era, mapping the platform's strategic shift from US-centric content to a global production powerhouse.

## Key Findings

- Identified that the library remains **70% movie-heavy** despite global audience shifts toward episodic content.
- Uncovered International Dramas as the **primary engine for non-US growth**, indicating a deliberate strategy of using cinema as a market-entry vehicle.
- Mapped regional production hubs and their contribution to Netflix's subscriber growth milestones.
