## Overview

Designed a full mobile app UI/UX experience for a fresh vegetable marketplace, optimizing for discoverability, conversion, and a delightful shopping experience.

## Design Highlights

- Created **reusable product cards** with strategic calls-to-action (Add to Cart, Quick View) to increase conversion rates.
- Designed a **5-tab bottom navigation bar** for instant access to Home, Search, Cart, Orders, and Profile.
- Planned **intuitive checkout banners and promotional sliders** to maximize average order value.
- Applied a fresh, vibrant color palette aligned with the brand's natural, farm-to-table identity.

Figma · UI Design · Digital Branding · Prototyping · Motion Design
