## Overview

Designed an end-to-end UI/UX system for a multi-service hospital platform, covering patient onboarding, service discovery, and conversion-optimized call-to-action flows.

## Design Highlights

- Created a **custom card-based modular service grid** allowing easy future expansion for categories like Medical Tourism and Specialist Referrals.
- Integrated **social login onboarding** to lower friction barriers for new policyholders and partners.
- Strategically placed **"Know More" and "Login" CTAs** at key decision points to guide users toward conversion.
- Leveraged custom illustrations to visually differentiate user types (patients, partners, staff) across the platform.
